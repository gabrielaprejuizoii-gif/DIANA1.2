Homework: take the adventure home

Main: my Halloween character

Invent a witch, vampire, ghost, monster or something new. Draw and color it in your notebook. Write 5 to 7 sentences: name, looks, personality, clothes, powers, home, likes.

Example (do not copy!): Her name is Luna. She is a young witch. She has long purple hair. She lives in a haunted castle. She loves chocolate!

Optional: Halloween photo challenge

Find Halloween-looking things at home, or draw a spooky scene. Write 5 sentences with: There is… There are… It is… It has… It is next to…

Example: There is a pumpkin on the table. There are two bats near the window.

Teacher’s guide (45 min)

Goals

  • Use Halloween vocabulary in real sentences.
  • Practice there is/are, prepositions, I would, descriptions and questions.
  • Speak spontaneously in roleplays; connect ideas with then, because, finally.

Materials

Paper, colored pencils, notebook, timer, optional candy.

Timing (45 min)

  • Warm-up 4 · Vocabulary 8 · Expressions 3
  • Costume 7 · Haunted house 6 · Games 4
  • Roleplays 7 · Story 3 · Party challenge 2 · Homework 1

Answers

  • True/false: TRUE, FALSE, TRUE, FALSE, TRUE (vampire and garlic: true in the legends).
  • Haunted house: three bats in the attic; the cat is under the chair; the ghost is behind the door.

Method

  • Roleplays: you are the host, neighbor or detective. Improvise from her answers; ask “why?” and “what happens next?”.
  • Correct by recasting: she says “I wear a witch”, you say “Oh, you’re dressed as a witch!”. Note errors and review at the end.
  • Fast finisher: she invents and describes a monster out loud, or swaps roles and asks you the questions.
  • Short on time: keep costume, haunted house, roleplay A and B, story.
